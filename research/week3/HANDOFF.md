## Phase H statistical analysis and BI sensitivity complete

- `phase_h_analysis.py` consumes only frozen local Phase G/Week 2 artifacts;
  it makes no API calls and requires no GPU.
- Statistical inference uses the 33 documents as paired units. It reports
  10,000-sample percentile bootstrap 95% confidence intervals, two-sided
  Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Pratt handling for zeros, and Holm correction
  across the nine Adaptive-versus-fixed comparisons within each metric family.
- The original locked Adaptive decisions are reproduced exactly before any
  ablation. The page-neutral sensitivity sets BI weight to zero and equally
  weights SC, ICC, DCC, and RC.
- The boundary audit confirms 1,730/1,732 page boundaries (99.88%) coincide
  with parser split points within five characters. Removing BI changes 9/33
  selections: page drops from 15 to 9 documents and `our_recurs_1100` rises
  from 13 to 20.
- For nDCG@10, no Adaptive-versus-fixed comparison remains significant at
  Holm-adjusted 0.05. Adaptive minus raw `langch_recurs_1100` is -0.0504
  (95% document-bootstrap CI [-0.1006, -0.0035]) but adjusted p=0.3266.
- Counterfactual selector replay changes nDCG@10 from 0.7475 to 0.7626
  (+0.0151; 95% CI [-0.0109, 0.0460]; adjusted p=0.4444). This replay is a
  diagnostic only, not an exact mixed-index retrieval result.
- Outputs are under `artifacts/phase-h-analysis/`; `SUMMARY.md` separates paper
  claims, code/artifact evidence, and analyst inference. The Phase H manifest
